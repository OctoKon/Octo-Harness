## Candidate implications

The material adds three missing product shapes to the corpus: (1) **agent hosts** that provide a terminal/editor plus orchestration (Warp), (2) **one harness across local/headless/ACP surfaces** (Grok Build and ZCode), and (3) **fresh-VM asynchronous agents** (Jules). It also sharpens a lifecycle rule: fork/archival/successor status must be a first-class adapter precondition, not an incidental documentation note.

## Remaining evidence gaps

1. Run protocol conformance and cancellation/teardown checks only in a later controlled evaluation; documentation does not prove them.
2. Obtain provider policy sources for hosted/closed products and third-party channel adapters before handling real repository or credential data.
3. Review supply-chain/update paths before allowing registry-driven launchers, package/tap installers or forked distributions.
