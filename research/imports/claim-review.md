# Claim review for the supplied compendium

## Scope

This review indexes a public user-supplied synthesis, preserved in [the imported PDF](ai-coding-harnesses-and-scaffolds.pdf) and its [site companion](../site/index.html). The PDF has 63 pages and was extracted with `pypdf` on 2026-10-25. The import traces to public commit [`04e7613`](https://github.com/OctoKon/Splyce/commit/04e7613b7f4fb1697bcc93fb5130110316433afe). It is secondary material: it supplies hypotheses and pointers, but does not independently verify them.

No statement below calls a supplied assertion false. Each marked hypothesis lacks the version-pinned primary source, reproducible method, or executed conformance evidence needed to use it as a factual product comparison. Existing research is documentation-verified only and is linked where it bears on the question.
