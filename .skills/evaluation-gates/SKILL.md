# Evaluation gates

Use the selected work package and qualification record. Do not choose a winner from a feature table or a demonstration.

1. Freeze the baseline/candidate revisions, independent task/session/request unit, strata, held-out inputs, model/template, authority, hardware, budgets and grader before trials. Record the primary outcome, justified quality margin, sample/precision target, selection budget, stopping rule and treatment of missing/canceled runs.
2. Grade the final environment and cited evidence, not an agent's success statement. Verify that removing the mechanism makes its decisive test fail. Protect evaluator and holdout state from the candidate.
3. For a recommended combination, bind the selected closure/configuration/artifact in the existing qualification record. Compare base/full/preregistered targeted removals, preserve mandatory safety controls, and declare eligible interaction/ordered-transition coverage and omissions. Reopen affected evidence after relevant changes; do not gate base delivery on optional recommendation trials.
4. Run matched trials from clean isolated state. Separate cold/warm/cache and single/concurrent workloads. Keep deterministic contract faults distinct from actual-model quality and installed-platform tests.
5. Retain every trial, failure and inconclusive result with receipts. Count extraction, embedding, reranking, indexing, refresh, idle service and recovery costs, not generation alone. Report paired outcomes and uncertainty; unavailable data has no numeric result.
6. A policy, scope, deletion or recovery failure blocks admission regardless of average score. A quality-preserving benefit is conditional on the measured workload; use one untouched confirmation after candidate selection.

Write the disposition and recheck trigger in the qualification register. An unrun experiment remains n=0. Do not tune a threshold after seeing its result or hide an unfavorable stratum.
